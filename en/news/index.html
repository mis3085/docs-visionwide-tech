Company Updates and Notable Events
#

Vision Wide Tech Co., Ltd. has marked several important milestones and achievements throughout its history. Below is a curated summary of key news and events that have shaped the company’s journey.

2025 Factory Opening and New Product Launch
#

  • 15th Anniversary & New Nantou Plant Opening
    Vision Wide celebrated its 15th anniversary with the opening of a new plant in Nantou. The event was held at No. 1126-2, Bishan Rd., Caotun Township, Nantou 54258, Taiwan.
  • Beam-Raising Ceremony
    The beam-raising ceremony for the Nantou Factory was a significant highlight, marking a new chapter in the company’s expansion.
